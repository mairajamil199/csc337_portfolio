My Favourite Games

Online Game: Roblox

Roblox is an online platform where players can play millions of games created by other users, or build their own using Roblox Studio. I enjoy exploring different game worlds, playing with friends and trying creative experiences. It also teaches basic game design and logical thinking, and it is a fun way to stay connected with friends online.

Physical Game: Badminton

Badminton is a racket sport played with a shuttlecock, either one against one (singles) or two against two (doubles). Players hit the shuttlecock over a net and try to land it in the opponent's court. I play with friends in my free time. It improves my fitness, reflexes and concentration, and doubles teaches teamwork.
